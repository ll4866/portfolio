ABOUT ME

LUCAS LIN

INTERACTION & EXPERIENCE DESIGNER

I am an Experience & Interaction Designer with a background in Interactive Media Arts at New York University Shanghai. As a fourth-generation Chinese Brazilian, my education across Brazil, the United States, and China has shaped my understanding of how people interact with technology, environments, and each other.

From studying at a bilingual German school in Brazil, continuing my education in the United States, and later pursuing my degree in Shanghai, I have developed a multicultural perspective that influences my approach to design. I am interested in creating experiences that connect people, technology, and culture, whether through interactive systems, digital experiences, or everyday interactions.
